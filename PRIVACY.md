# Privacy

Pacemark does not collect, send or sell any data.

- Documents you open are read entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- Your reading speed, highlight color and other settings are stored locally (browser storage, or
  Chrome's sync storage for the extension, which Chrome syncs between your own signed-in browsers).
- Reading positions are stored only in your browser.
- The extension runs on a page only when you start it (toolbar button, shortcut or context menu).
  When you ask it to open a PDF from a website, it requests access to that one site so it can download the file.
- The web app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Everything else ships with the app.
